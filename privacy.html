Privacy Policy

Last updated: 5 November 2026

1. Who operates this site

This is a personal portfolio operated by Lakshit (also known as Lavi), an individual based in India. It is not an online store and does not offer account registration or user profiles.

Privacy questions or requests can be sent to lakshitsinghsaini@gmail.com.

2. Information this site collects

The portfolio's own front-end code does not ask visitors to submit personal information. There is no contact form, account, newsletter signup, payment form, analytics tag, advertising pixel or embedded third-party application in the current version.

The site stores one preference in your browser's local storage: whether the wallpaper theme is enabled or disabled. This value is used only to remember your visual preference. It is not sent to the site owner by the page code.

3. Hosting and technical logs

This site is hosted using GitHub Pages. GitHub and its infrastructure providers may process technical information such as IP address, browser/device details, request timestamps and server/security logs to deliver, protect and operate the hosting service. Those activities are governed by GitHub's own terms and privacy information, not controlled by this portfolio's front-end code. See GitHub Pages documentation and GitHub's Privacy Statement.

4. Third-party resources and links

The technology-stack section currently requests icon images from jsDelivr (Devicon assets) and Simple Icons. When your browser loads these images, those providers may receive technical request data such as your IP address and browser information. External social, repository and email links take you to services with their own privacy practices. This site does not control those services.

No third-party video, social feed, map, advertising or analytics widget is embedded in the page.

5. Purpose and choices

The local theme preference is used to remember your display choice. Hosting logs are handled by the hosting provider for service delivery and security. You can clear this site's local storage through your browser settings; doing so resets the wallpaper preference.

6. Retention and security

The site owner does not maintain a visitor database through this static website. Hosting-provider logs and records are retained under the provider's policies. Reasonable care is taken when maintaining the site, but no internet transmission or storage system can be guaranteed completely secure.

7. Your privacy requests

You can contact lakshitsinghsaini@gmail.com with a privacy question or request concerning information you believe this site owner controls. Please do not send passwords, government identifiers or other sensitive information by email. Requests relating to hosting logs or third-party services may need to be directed to the relevant provider.

8. India and the DPDP Act

This notice is intended to describe the site's actual, limited data practices transparently. India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and the DPDP Rules, 2025 have a phased commencement schedule. The applicability and commencement of individual obligations depend on the notified dates and the facts of the processing. This page is not a legal opinion or a claim of certified compliance. The site will be reviewed as the relevant provisions take effect.

Official references: DPDP Act, 2023 and DPDP Rules, 2025.

9. Changes

This notice may be updated when the website's features, hosting or applicable legal requirements change. The date at the top indicates the latest revision.
