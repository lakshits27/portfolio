Cookie Policy

Last updated: 5 November 2026

1. Current use of cookies

The current portfolio source does not set or read browser cookies for analytics, advertising or marketing. It does not include an analytics provider, advertising network or tracking pixel.

2. Local storage preference

The site uses the browser's localStorage key itznan_wallpaper_theme to remember whether the wallpaper theme is enabled. This is browser storage, not a cookie. It supports a feature you actively use and is not used to identify you or track activity across websites. You can clear it using your browser's site-data controls.

3. Third-party resources

Technology icon images are requested from jsDelivr and Simple Icons. These are external image requests rather than embedded applications. The providers may receive ordinary technical request information. See the Privacy Policy for details.

4. Is a consent banner needed?

Based on the current source-code review, this site does not use non-essential analytics or advertising cookies, so a cookie-consent banner is not currently implemented or indicated by the site's own code. This is not a guarantee about cookies or logs that GitHub Pages or a visitor's browser extensions may use. Reassess before adding analytics, advertising, tracking cookies or other non-essential storage. Where consent is legally required, obtain it before activating the relevant technology and provide a way to change or withdraw the choice.

5. Changes

This policy will be revised if the site starts using cookies or other tracking/storage technologies beyond the current theme preference.
